Hello everyone, and welcome to English for Earth. My name is Mostafa, and I am very happy that you are listening to the first episode of this podcast. Before we start talking about environmental issues, I would like to introduce this project and explain why I decided to create it. English for Earth is a podcast that combines two things that are very important to me: learning English and caring for our planet. Many people believe that environmental problems are too big for ordinary people to solve. When we hear about climate change(tempature rise in globe), pollution(a material that can harm nature), or deforestation(cutting all trees of a jungle), we often think that only governments or large organizations can make a difference. But we should see it differently. I believe that change often starts with small actions. There is a famous saying: "To move a mountain, start by carrying away small stones." This idea inspired the today's title: One Stone at a Time. Imagine a huge mountain. No single person can move it in one day. However, if people keep removing small stones, day after day, the mountain will slowly change. Environmental problems are similar. One person cannot stop climate change alone. One person cannot clean every ocean. One person cannot remove all pollution. But one person can start. One person can learn. One person can teach others. And one person can inspire change. That is why this podcast exists. In every episode, we will explore topics related to the environment, sustainability, and global challenges while also improving our English skills. You will hear useful vocabulary, interesting ideas, and real-world topics that can help you become a more confident English speaker. At the same time, I hope these conversations encourage us to think about the world around us. This podcast is not about being perfect. It is not about blaming people. And it is not about telling others what to do. Instead, it is about learning together. Because every positive change starts with awareness. When we understand a problem, we are more likely to take action. Maybe after listening to an episode, someone will learn a new English word. Maybe someone will recycle more often. Maybe someone will share an idea with a friend. Those actions may seem small, but small actions matter. One stone at a time. As this is the first episode, I would also like to thank you for joining me at the beginning of this journey. Whether you are listening from a big city, a small town, or somewhere in between, I am glad you are here. In future episodes, we will talk about plastic pollution, climate change, renewable energy, water conservation, and many other important topics. So if you enjoy learning English through meaningful discussions, I hope you will continue listening. Thank you for being here. This is English for Earth. And remember: big changes often begin with small actions, one stone at a time. See you in the next episode.
