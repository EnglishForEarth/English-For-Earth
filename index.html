Learn English. Protect the Planet.

EnglishForEarth is a student-led podcast that combines English learning with environmental awareness. Every episode introduces a real-world environmental topic while helping learners improve their vocabulary, listening skills, and confidence in English.

Why Eco English?

Environmental issues affect everyone, regardless of where they live. Through this project, I hope to make English learning more meaningful by connecting language with real-world topics that matter.
